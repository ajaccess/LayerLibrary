LayerLibrary

Version 0.3.0 · A portable home for your 3D printing projects.

Open one HTML file. Drop your folders. Browse, search, rotate models, and save a library to take with you.

No account, installation, uploads, analytics, cookies, or saved browser preferences. Models and previews stay in memory until you close the tab. Explicit downloads and saved HTML files remain on disk. Browser history and file permissions are controlled by your browser.

Supports 3MF, STL, OBJ, PLY, and AMF. Preview fidelity depends on the file: slicer settings, colors, textures, and vendor extensions may not be fully represented. 3MF stores meshes, not necessarily an embedded STL file.
